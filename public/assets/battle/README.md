# Battle flow artwork

Both assets were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for Diademfall. Frontier was a layout reference; its screenshots are not used as game assets.

- `dueling-floor.png` — match reveal, battlefield, and result screen.
- `draft-armory.png` — background for summoner and troop selection.

## Prompts

Use case: stylized-concept. Generate an original landscape 16:9 top-down orthographic fantasy card-battle arena floor for Diademfall. Camera directly overhead. A large dark warm walnut plank circular dueling floor viewed from above, centered circle of slate with a simple carved original broken crown sigil, bronze and pale stone ring at the perimeter, shallow engraved angular corners, tiny warm ember sconces at the extreme outer corners. Clean painted game environment, confident dark ink edges and simplified color shapes, subtle hand-painted wood grain. Side and top/bottom edges in darker shade with quiet uncluttered open space for landscape cards, center lit slightly more, enough contrast for card silhouettes. The floor should fill the whole horizontal image with the middle ring around 30 percent of width, no horizon, no tilted perspective. No characters, cards, logos, lettering, numerals, interface, arrows, or bright saturated light.

Use case: stylized-concept. Original 16:9 fantasy game backdrop for Diademfall's card draft screen, a warmly lit timber-and-stone warband armory, eye-level view. Quiet dark oak bookcases and folded banners at sides, softly lit iron-bound arched stone entrance toward right, copper lantern on the left and restrained warm amber illumination. Keep central area subdued, clear of bright focal points, so four columns of character cards and a dark sidebar can overlay it. Painterly fantasy game scenery with dark ink accents, bold shapes, and a modest hand-painted texture that complements cel-shaded character illustrations. No characters, no cards, no weapons floating, no UI, logos, signs, lettering, text or watermark.
