## Navigation atlas prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: transparent fantasy browser card game navigation atlas, exactly 4 columns by 3 rows, landscape canvas 4:3. One atlas with twelve distinct original symbols.
Primary request: replace overly ornate RPG inventory art with clean graphic storybook fantasy HUD art like Splinterlands navigation: strong expressive silhouettes, hand-drawn angular outlines, simple cel-painted color blocks, subtle edge highlights, 2D illustrated cartoon fantasy, minimal internal detail. Icons sit behind menu labels at 40px, so each symbol must read at tiny size. NOT photorealistic, NOT shiny rendered 3D objects, NOT ornate jewelry.
Exact row-major order:
Row 1: teal armored raised fist (PLAY); ochre canvas coin satchel (SHOP); violet merchant stall with canopy and scales (MARKET); forest green crown-topped pennant (LADDER).
Row 2: three warm amber coins overlapping (TREASURY); indigo open spellbook with cream pages and one cyan star (CODEX); bronze circular league medallion with simple crown (RANKED); pale steel shield with blue inset (PRACTICE).
Row 3: crossed orange-gold swords (CHALLENGE); icy cyan faceted crystal (CREDITS); three fanned ochre playing cards (COLLECTION); brown wooden treasure chest with teal glow (REWARDS).
Composition: perfectly equal square cells in a precise 4x3 grid, each icon centered with consistent 14% padding on ALL sides, each icon uses about 72% of cell width/height. No icon crosses a cell boundary. All objects fully visible. Consistent light from upper left. Restrained gold accents. No letters, labels, background tile, circle behind object except the ranked medallion, borders, grid lines, floor, watermark, or cast shadows.
Critical alpha: genuine fully transparent background everywhere outside crisp object contours, including all empty areas between objects and all corners; no semitransparent gray haze. Keep contours antialiased. Designed for a dark navy header.

## Crown emblem prompt

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: original compact circular crown emblem for the DIademfall fantasy card game navbar, transparent square PNG.
Primary request: a bold small game-brand medallion emblem with the instant readability and clean graphic weight of a fantasy browser card game navigation logo. A perfect circular midnight-black disk outlined by a thin warm gold ring, containing ONE iconic ivory-gold five-point royal crown. The crown is a simple broad powerful silhouette with three tallest pointed tips and two shorter outer tips, small diamond-shaped cuts, and one flat base. A tiny dark crack splitting the central tip suggests the shattered crown. Balanced left-right and front facing, immediately recognizable at 30px.
Style: 2D hand-drawn fantasy game emblem, crisp angular cartoon contours, restrained flat gold and ivory highlights, dark blue-black interior, no 3D bevels, no photoreal metal texture, no jewels, no wings, no ribbon, no surrounding flourishes.
Composition: centered circular emblem occupies 84% of square, generous uniform transparent outer padding. No text or letters. Black interior is opaque, outside ring is fully alpha-zero transparent with clean antialiased edges. No external shadow, no background square, no mockup, no watermark.
