# Release and balance rules

## Launch mana curve

Across Ancient and Oath, each Banner has 12 troops: one 0-mana, one 1-mana, two each at 2, 3, and 4 mana, then one each at 5, 6, 7, and 8 mana. Legionnaire adds six more troops per Banner at 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, and 9 mana. The six Banners provide 108 troops, and 12 shared Wayfarers bring the complete launch to 120 troops. The Banner pool includes 36 troops costing 0–2 mana, with additional low-cost Wayfarers.

## Cheap troops

- Each Banner has exactly one 0-mana troop at launch.
- A 0-mana troop occupies a formation slot and duplicate card IDs are illegal.
- The 0-mana troop begins with 1 health, no attack, and no level-1 ability; Banner modifiers never increase its starting health.
- Slots 0, 1, 6, and 8 in each Banner have no level-1 ability, giving exactly 24 ability-free launch troops.
- Legionnaire adds two more initially ability-free low-cost troops per Banner at launch.

## Pack visibility

- Ancient, Oath, and Legionnaire are all public launch packs.
- All 30 summoners, 108 Banner troops, and 12 Wayfarer troops may be imported into the production catalog at release.

## Balance boundary

The generated level curves use predictable integer growth and faction modifiers. They are coherent engine inputs, not a claim of final balance. Before release, simulate every summoner/ruleset combination at multiple mana caps and version any numerical changes through a new catalog snapshot.
