# Splinterlands mechanics reviewed

- Splinterlands currently uses Wood, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond, and Champion, with three tiers in each league except Wood. Advancement follows rating automatically. Source: https://docs.splinterlands.com/gameplay/leagues
- League/tier advancement rewards are earned the first time a tier is reached and chest quality increases at higher tiers. Chests must be opened on advancement rather than accumulated indefinitely. Source: https://support.splinterlands.com/hc/en-us/articles/30800654371604-League-Tier-Advancement-Chests-FAQ
- Its reward shop uses three chest grades with different reward quality. Source: https://docs.splinterlands.com/rewards/glint-rewards-shop
- Leaderboard qualification and prizes are separate from league advancement rewards. Source: https://support.splinterlands.com/hc/en-us/articles/30950100374292-Leaderboard-Prizes-Tie-Breakers

Diademfall adapts the progression structure but uses original thresholds, chest names, contents, probabilities, Credit values, Coin-share settlement, and a hard Legionnaire reserve.
