# Wayfarer rules

## Identity

Wayfarers are independent mercenaries, creatures, pilgrims, constructs, and sellswords. They have `affiliation=wayfarer` and `banner=null`. There is no Wayfarer summoner.

## Drafting

A legal standard team contains one summoner, troops from its Banner, and at most two Wayfarers. Wayfarers count toward the six-troop formation limit and mana cap. Duplicate card IDs remain illegal.

- `all_allies` includes Wayfarers.
- `banner_allies` and named-Banner selectors exclude Wayfarers.
- Wayfarers do not satisfy same-Banner formation conditions.
- A summoner may explicitly grant an ability to a Wayfarer if its selector includes that troop.

## Pack distribution

Each launch pack gains four Wayfarers. Ancient now has 46 cards; Oath and Legionnaire each have 52. The launch catalog is 150 cards: 30 summoners, 108 Banner troops, and 12 Wayfarers. Pack supply remains 500,000 per edition.
