# Commander support lineup

Every Commander costs at least 3 mana. Basic single effects cost 3 mana; stronger single effects cost 4; two-effect support always costs 6. Ancient and Oath Commanders may have up to two abilities. Legionnaire Commanders have exactly one. All effects are automatic and team-wide. Attack bonuses affect existing attack types only.
