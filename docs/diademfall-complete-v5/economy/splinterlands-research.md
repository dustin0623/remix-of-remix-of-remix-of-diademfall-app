# Splinterlands review and design lessons

This economy uses structural lessons, not copied names or exact payouts.

1. Credits are nontransferable and use 1,000 units per USD. Source: https://support.splinterlands.com/hc/en-us/articles/13779338188948-Intro-to-Splinterlands-Tokens
2. Ranked rewards have used pool/share systems rather than a permanently fixed payout per victory. Current Modern rewards use a player's Reward Points divided by total eligible Reward Points in a bracket. Source: https://support.splinterlands.com/hc/en-us/articles/6614462185620-How-Are-Rewards-Calculated-in-Modern-Ranked
3. Historical ranked reward factors included league, rating/reward shares, win streaks, foil/set bonuses, and other multipliers. Source: https://docs.splinterlands.com/rewards/ranked-battle-rewards
4. Leaderboards have used minimum battle requirements and rating, win ratio, streak, and total wins as tie breakers. Sources: https://support.splinterlands.com/hc/en-us/articles/30950100374292-Leaderboard-Prizes-Tie-Breakers and https://docs.splinterlands.com/rewards/leaderboard-rewards
5. Packs contain five cards, guarantee at least one Rare-or-better card in the historical four-rarity model, and are sold while supply lasts. Source: https://docs.splinterlands.com/cards/booster-packs/purchasing-booster-packs
6. Historical standard pulls used a 2% Gold chance and Gold cards supplied reward-share bonuses. Source: https://docs.splinterlands.com/cards/gold-foil-cards
7. Historical editions used predefined pack caps, including 300,000 Alpha and 900,000 Beta packs. Source: https://support.splinterlands.com/hc/en-us/articles/4412517301140-Terms-of-Service
8. A finite pack run fixes edition-wide card output, but individual card supply remains probabilistic until packs open. Source: https://docs.splinterlands.com/cards/card-supply
9. Card rentals and marketplace activity provide token sinks and owner income. Source: https://support.splinterlands.com/hc/en-us/articles/5574634792468-How-to-Rent-Cards

Diademfall deliberately simplifies this to Credits plus Coins, introduces an Uncommon rarity, reduces the Gold reward multiplier, makes all gameplay emissions hard-capped, and launches all three packs simultaneously.
